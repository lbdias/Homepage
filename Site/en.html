About me

Hello, my name is Leticia, and I am currently studying Computer Science! My journey in programming is relatively recent, as for many years I was convinced that I would become an architect (and I came very close), but life takes us down different paths.
I had to completely change my life and left my Architecture and Urban Planning degree during my 7th semester, as I had to move to Portugal and would not be able to continue my studies there. So, after thinking carefully and reflecting on my life and the world we live in, I decided that Computer Science would be a great choice, as it is a growing field with many opportunities.
I am still at the beginning of my journey, as my current goal is to first become a Front-End Developer, but I intend to eventually become a Full-Stack Developer.
I like to carry quotes with me throughout life, and there are two that have always inspired me: 

"Life is a breath"

I heard this quote spoken by Oscar Niemeyer during my time studying architecture, and it made me realize that life is short and that we should make the most of every moment. 
"If you're afraid, you can't see the beauty. Fear kills your ability to see beauty. Fear ruins life."

I don't know exactly who originally said either of these quotes, but I heard the second one in a video, and it made me reflect on how fear can prevent us from enjoying life and its opportunities, and that we should move forward even when we are afraid.

Tech Curiosidades

Project developed during my Front-End Development studies through Curso em Vídeo, focusing on the practical application of HTML5 and CSS3.
The project consists of a responsive website about the history of the Android mascot, using different structure and styling elements to create an organized and visually appealing page.

  • Page structuring with HTML5;
  • Styling and organization of layouts with CSS3;
  • Use of images, links and multimedia content;
  • Creation of a simple and intuitive navigation;
  • Semantic organization of content;
  • Development of a responsive layout for different screen sizes.

Cordel Moderno

Second project developed during my Front-End Development studies through Curso em Vídeo, focusing on the practical application of HTML5 and CSS3
The project consists of creating a web page to present the cordel "Cordel Moderno", using different styling and positioning techniques to create a pleasant visual experience adapted to different screen sizes.
Main concepts applied:

  • Semantic page structuring with HTML5;
  • Styling and customization of elements with CSS3;
  • Work with fonts, colors, spacing and positioning;
  • Use of images as part of the visual composition;
  • Responsive layout development;
  • Content organization to improve the reading experience.
